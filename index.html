About Our Emergency Directory

The Community Emergency Resource Directory connects citizens, disaster relief volunteers, and families with verified emergency relief centers during floods, earthquakes, or medical crises.

During emergencies, clean drinking water (H2O) and medical kits with safety level 10+ are distributed.
Remember: For immediate life-saving assistance, dial 112 directly.

Emergency Services & Action Steps

Available Relief Services:

  • 24/7 Critical Trauma Hospital Care & Ambulances
  • Disaster Safe Shelters with Clean Beds & Blankets
  • Food Relief Kitchens & Packaged Drinking Water

Steps to Follow in an Emergency:

  1. Move to an open ground or the nearest community shelter.
  2. Contact the local center helpline from the table below.
  3. Follow instructions issued by official disaster relief workers.
