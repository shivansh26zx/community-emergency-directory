About Our Directory

The Community Emergency Resource Directory helps citizens quickly find safe relief camps, trauma hospitals, and free food during crises.

During flood or disaster alerts, safe drinking water (H2O) and medical kits with score 10+ are supplied. Always remember: Safety is our top priority!

Available Relief Services

  • 24/7 Emergency Medical Aid & Trauma Care
  • Disaster Shelter Camps with Clean Bedding
  • Clean Food & Packaged Drinking Water Distribution

Emergency Action Steps

  1. Check your family's safety immediately.
  2. Call national helpline 112 if urgent medical care is needed.
  3. Locate the nearest emergency center in the table below.
