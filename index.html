About Our Directory

Welcome to the Community Emergency Resource Directory. This website helps citizens quickly find safe emergency shelters, hospitals, and clean drinking water during disasters.

Always remember: In any life-threatening emergency, call 112 immediately.


Emergency Services & Action Steps

Available Relief Services:

  • 24/7 Emergency Hospital & Medical Care
  • Temporary Evacuation Shelters & Beds
  • Clean Drinking Water & Food Supplies

Steps to Follow in an Emergency:

  1. Move to a safe open ground or community shelter.
  2. Contact the nearest helpline from the schedule below.
  3. Follow instructions given by disaster relief officers.
