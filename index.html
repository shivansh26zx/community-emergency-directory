2. Essential Relief Protocols & Supplies

Emergency Supply Kit Essentials (Unordered List)

  • Clean Drinking Water (At least 3 liters per person per day)
  • Non-perishable ready-to-eat food packets
  • Certified First-Aid medical kit & prescription medicines
  • Battery-powered flashlights with extra batteries
  • Emergency whistle & battery-operated radio

Four Steps to Follow in a Crisis (Ordered List)

  1. Assess Immediate Safety: Move away from hazards, structural damage, or fire.
  2. Contact Emergency Helplines: Call 112 or designated nodal centers.
  3. Proceed to Nearest Evacuation Zone: Refer to the table below for active relief grounds.
  4. Register with Directory Volunteers: Record personal details for family reunification.

Crisis Terminology & Definitions (Description List)

Triage
The process of determining the priority of patients' treatments based on the severity of their condition.
Disaster Shelter
A temporary facility equipped with bedding, sanitation, and food rations for displaced individuals.
Trauma Relief Wing
A dedicated critical-care unit staffed with specialized emergency surgeons and triage doctors.
